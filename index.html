Gameplay

An idle game you actually play

You are not watching from above: you are the one on the sand, running between the counter and the sunbeds while the coins pile up.

1

Serve with one thumb

Walk up to the counter, stack what your guests ask for and bring it to them before their patience runs out. The more you carry, the fewer trips you make.

  • Sunbeds
  • Drinks
  • Gelato
  • Granita
  • Towels
2

Beat the Rush

At noon the crowd arrives and everything speeds up. Chain your deliveries, keep every guest happy and watch the tips and the multiplier climb. Miss too many and the reviews will let you know.

  • Midday Rush
  • Tips
  • Streak multiplier
3

Build, hire, renovate

Unlock one zone after the other: drink kiosk, gelateria, granita kiosk, pedalo pier, beach volley, restaurant terrace. Hire a barista and beach helpers who work alongside you, then renovate each beach up to level 5, with a new zone at every level.

  • Games corner
  • Dog area
  • Hot tub
  • Canopy beds

A chain across Italy

Five beaches, five Italian summers

Open new beach clubs on the map of Italy. Every region has its own coast, its own food and its own way of having fun.

  1. 1

    Rimini Emilia-Romagna

    Wide golden beach · gelato and pedal boats

    Only here: liscio dancing under pink lights, then fireworks.

  2. 2

    Porto Cervo Sardinia

    Coves among the granite · masks and fritto

    Only here: sailboats in the cove, fritto for the fans.

  3. 3

    Sanremo Liguria

    Seafront and pebbles · focaccia and pesto

    Only here: the pesto contest, focaccia for the fans.

  4. 4

    Positano Campania

    Pebbles below the village · wood-fired pizza

    Only here: a serenade from a boat, lemonade for the couples.

  5. 5

    Tropea Calabria

    White sand below the cliff · tartufo ice cream

    Only here: a dance round the bonfire, ’nduja and then a drink.

More of the coast is already on the map, marked “coming soon”.

Reviews, stars and perks

Your guests have opinions

Every evening your guests review the day, and they do not hold back. These lines come straight from the game: the guests are made up, the honesty is not.

Stars and perks

Good service earns stars, and stars buy perks that follow you to every beach.

Earn while you are away

Your beaches keep earning while the game is closed. Come back, collect and decide what to build next.

Short or long sessions

A few minutes on the bus or a whole afternoon on the sofa: the beach waits for you.

FAQ

Good questions

Is Riviera Rush free?

Yes. Riviera Rush is free to download and play. It shows ads, and you can watch optional videos for bonuses.

Is Riviera Rush on iPhone?

Yes. Riviera Rush is free on the App Store for iPhone, and on Google Play for Android.

Do I need an account to play?

No. There is no login and nothing to sign up for: your progress is saved on your phone.

Which languages is Riviera Rush in?

English and Italian.

Which Italian beaches are in the game?

Rimini, Porto Cervo, Sanremo, Positano and Tropea, each with its own look, food and summer event. More of the coast is already on the map, marked as coming soon.

Who makes Riviera Rush?

Claudio Pellegrino, an indie developer from Italy: game design, code and release are all done by the same person. Have a look at the other apps by Claudio Pellegrino.

Found a bug or have an idea?

Write to rivierarush.game@gmail.com. Every message is read.
