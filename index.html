Riviera Rush

Run your beach club on the Italian coast.

In testing — coming to Google Play and the App Store

Sunbeds to carry, umbrellas to open, granitas to serve and a queue that never ends. Riviera Rush is an arcade idle game about running the beach clubs of Italy: you start with one strip of sand in Rimini and end up with a chain of lidos along the whole coast.

What you do

One thumb, no waiting

Pick things up, drop them where they belong, collect the coins. Everything happens while you move — no menus in the way.

The Rush

At noon the beach fills up. Keep everyone served while the patience bars drain, and the day closes with three stars.

A chain across Italy

Open lidos in real towns — Rimini, Sanremo, Positano, Tropea, Porto Cervo — each with its own look, food and habits.

It grows while you watch

Every coin you spend changes the sand in front of you: a new row, a kiosk, a helper who runs so you don't have to.

Name your own lido

Each beach club is yours to name, and the guests leave reviews about it — some kinder than others.

Plays offline

No account, no login, nothing to sign up for. Your progress stays on your phone.
