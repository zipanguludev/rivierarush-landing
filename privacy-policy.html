Privacy Policy

pursuant to Article 13 of Regulation (EU) 2016/679 ("GDPR")

Effective date: September 23, 2026 · Versione italiana

1. Scope

This policy describes the processing of personal data in connection with the use of the mobile application Riviera Rush (application id com.zipangulu.lidorush), available for Android and iOS and published by Claudio Pellegrino.

2. Controller

The controller is Claudio Pellegrino, who publishes the application (the "Controller"), and who can be contacted at zipangulu.dev@gmail.com.

3. Data processed and purposes

3.1 Data stored on the device. The application stores the user's game progress and preferences in the app's private storage on the device. This data is not transmitted to the Controller or to third parties and is deleted when the application is uninstalled. The application does not require an account and does not collect identifying data such as name, email address or contacts.

3.2 Advertising. The application is free and is funded by advertising provided by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited). For the delivery, measurement and fraud prevention of ads, the following may be processed: the device's advertising identifier; the IP address, from which an approximate location may be derived; technical information about the device and the application; data relating to interactions with ads. On iOS the advertising identifier is used only if the user allows it through Apple's tracking request.

3.3 Notifications. If the user allows them, the application schedules local notifications, generated by the device itself, which involve no transmission of data.

4. Legal basis and consent

For users located in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the processing of data for personalised advertising is based on the user's consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR), collected at first launch through Google's consent management platform, before the advertising services are activated. In the absence of consent, ads with limited data processing may be shown, in accordance with Google's policies.

The user may change or withdraw consent at any time from the application's menu, under "Privacy settings". Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it. The advertising identifier can also be reset or deleted in the device settings.

5. Recipients and transfers

The data referred to in section 3.2 is processed by Google as an independent controller, in accordance with its own policies: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy. Google may also process the data outside the European Economic Area, with the safeguards provided for by the GDPR.

The application is distributed through Google Play and through Apple's App Store and TestFlight, which process their own data about downloads, installations and, where the user has allowed it, errors and performance, in accordance with their respective policies. Within testing programmes, the platform provides the Controller with the email address the user joined with, which is used solely to manage the test.

6. Retention

The Controller does not keep users' personal data on its own systems. Data processed by Google is retained in accordance with Google's policies. Email addresses of test participants are kept for the duration of the testing programme.

7. Children

The application is not intended for children under 13, and the Controller does not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Rights of the data subject

Users have the right to request access to their personal data, its rectification or erasure, the restriction of processing and data portability, to object to processing and to withdraw consent at any time, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali, www.garanteprivacy.it). For data processed by Google, these rights may also be exercised directly with Google. Requests to the Controller can be sent to zipangulu.dev@gmail.com.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated, in particular when new services are introduced. The updated version is published at this address, with its effective date, before the version of the application concerned is distributed.
