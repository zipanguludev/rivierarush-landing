Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

Leggi questa informativa in italiano

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Riviera Rush (application id com.zipangulu.lidorush), developed and published by an independent developer ("we", "us"). It describes what happens to data when you play the game on Android or iOS, and the choices you have.

Summary

  • The game requires no account and no registration. We never ask for your name, email address or contacts.
  • The game does not connect to the internet. It contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and it sends nothing anywhere.
  • Everything the game knows about you — your progress and your settings — is stored only on your device.
  • We operate no servers and hold no database about players. There is simply no personal data of yours in our hands.

What the game stores on your device

Riviera Rush saves, in the app's own private storage on your phone:

  • your progress: coins, the lidos you own and the names you gave them, unlocked areas, upgrades, stars and perks, day and streak counters;
  • your settings: music, sound effects, vibration, notifications, and the language you chose.

These files never leave the device. They are removed when you uninstall the game, and you can wipe them at any time by clearing the app's data in your system settings.

Notifications

If you allow them, the game schedules local reminders (for example when a building site you started is finished). They are created and delivered by your phone itself: no notification, and no information about you, is sent to us or to anyone else. You can turn them off at any time in the game's options or in your system settings.

What the game does not do

To be explicit, this version of Riviera Rush contains:

  • no advertising and no advertising SDK, and it does not read or use your device's advertising identifier;
  • no analytics and no usage tracking;
  • no crash reporting service;
  • no in-app purchases;
  • no network requests of any kind. The game works exactly the same with the phone in airplane mode.

On Android the operating system still lists the standard internet permission, because it is added automatically to every app built with the Unity engine. The game makes no use of it.

Data handled by the stores, not by us

Riviera Rush is distributed through Google Play and Apple's App Store and TestFlight. Those platforms process their own data about downloads, installations and, if you have enabled it in your device settings, crash and performance reports. That processing is governed by their policies, not by this one: Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy. During the testing phase, the store of your choice also shows us the email address you used to join the test, because that is how a testing programme works; we use it only to let you in, and for nothing else.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. Since it collects no data at all, no data about children is collected either.

Your rights

Where EU law applies, you have the right to access, rectify or erase your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali). In practice we hold no personal data of yours to access or erase: everything is on your own device, under your control.

Changes to this policy

The game will grow. When advertising, analytics or in-app purchases are added, this page will be rewritten to describe exactly what they process — and it will be updated before that version reaches you, with a new effective date at the top. The address of this page will not change.

Contact

For any question about privacy or about this policy, write to zipangulu.dev@gmail.com.
